Research

Cardiac segmentation
single ventricle
ISMRM 2027 · submitted

SV-MRSeg: detector-prompted SAM2 for label-efficient segmentation of single-ventricle cine CMR

SAM2 · YOLO · congenital heart disease

Patients with single-ventricle physiology need surgical palliation and lifelong cardiac MRI, and their anatomy varies too much for networks trained on biventricular hearts. Contouring cine images by hand is slow, and usually only the end-diastolic and end-systolic frames get traced. SV-MRSeg carries SimLVSeg's sparse-label training over to cine CMR, swaps its 3D U-Net for the SAM2 foundation-model encoder, and prompts SAM2 with boxes from a YOLO detector so segmentation runs fully automatically.

MR reconstruction
4D flow
MICCAI 2026 · CMRx4DFlow arXiv paper code

Joint supervised and self-supervised training for accelerated 4D flow MRI

unrolled networks

In 4D flow MRI the diagnostic signal is the phase difference between velocity encodings, so a reconstruction can look right in magnitude and still get the flow wrong. We extend a per-encoding variational network (FlowVN) with joint supervised and self-supervised training (JSSL) on both magnitude and velocity, plus acquisition-robust and acceleration-conditioned training, so one model covers R = 10–50 on 1.5 T and 3 T aortic scans.

On the CMRx4DFlow 2026 data, compared with a training-matched baseline, it cuts relative velocity error by 38–50% and angular error by 7.0–8.7°, and improves every held-out subject at every acceleration. On out-of-distribution data, JSSL raises SSIM by 7.2% and lowers angular and relative error by 38% and 31%.

Point-of-care diagnostics
Rice University
Sensors & Diagnostics 2025 paper code

Smartphone reading of CRISPR-Cas13 lateral flow assays

MobileNet

CRISPR-Cas13 lateral flow strips are cheap, but reading faint test lines by eye is error-prone. I collected 350+ phone photos of 12 lateral flow devices across multiple sites and augmented them into 3,500+ negative and 4,500+ positive strip images. U-Net and MobileNet-based networks segment the strip (95% accuracy across resolutions, contrast and lighting), and ClassNet, a lightweight classifier on the segmentation mask, calls the result: 97.7% accuracy on 2,400+ held-out images, with sensitivity above 98.2% and specificity above 96.5%.

Oral cancer screening
Rice University
SPIE Translational Biophotonics 2024 paper

Mobile Detection of Oral Cancer (mDOC)

Android · Grad-CAM++

mDOC is a smartphone device in a 3D-printed case that captures white-light and 405 nm autofluorescence images of the oral cavity. I built lightweight segmentation networks (U-Net with MobileNetV2/V3 encoders) that outline the oral mucosa, and a MobileNetV3 referral classifier on the masked image pairs with Grad-CAM++ attention maps. The whole pipeline runs in an Android app, so a referral decision comes back within 10 minutes of imaging.

ISMRM Workshop on PET/MRI 2023 poster 16

Attenuation correction of fNIRS optic fibers for PET reconstruction

C++ · uPMR790

On a combined PET/MR/fNIRS system, the fNIRS optical fibers sit in the PET field of view and attenuate photons that the reconstruction does not account for. I developed an image-processing pipeline that corrects this fiber attenuation during PET reconstruction, tested it on human data from 10 volunteers, and reduced the homogeneity deviation on a barrel-source phantom with fibers by 90%. The algorithm was implemented in C++ for the uPMR790 PET/MR scanner.
